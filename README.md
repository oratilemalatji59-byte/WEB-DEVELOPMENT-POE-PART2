# WEB-DEVELOPMENT-POE-PART2

1. Executive Summary
This proposal outlines the initiation and planning approach for the Glaze Cosmetics website — a five-page site (Home, Shades, Products, About, Shop) built to introduce the Glaze lip gloss range, showcase the nine-shade collection, and let customers place orders online.
The project is scoped as an 8-week engagement, moving from discovery and design through build, content loading, testing, and launch. This document defines the objectives, scope, timeline, budget, roles, and risks so that all parties are aligned before work begins.
2. Project Background
Glaze Cosmetics is a small lip gloss brand offering three formulas — Core Gloss, Plumping Gloss, and Tinted Lip Oil — across nine named shades. The brand currently has no dedicated website, which limits its ability to present the full product range, communicate its formulation story, and accept orders outside of in-person or social-media sales.
A dedicated website will give Glaze a permanent, on-brand home online: a place to introduce the shine-and-comfort formula story, let customers browse and compare shades, and submit orders directly.
3. Objectives
• Launch a five-page website that reflects the Glaze brand identity (colour palette, typography, tone of voice).
• Present all nine shades clearly, with names and short descriptions, so customers can choose confidently.
• Explain the three product formulas and their differences in a way that supports purchase decisions.
• Communicate the brand story (formulation approach, cruelty-free/vegan positioning) to build trust.
• Provide a working order-request form that captures formula, shade, and customer details.
• Deliver a site that works well on both mobile and desktop, since most traffic is expected from social media.
4. Scope of Work
4.1 In Scope
• Home page with hero section and navigation into the rest of the site.
• Shades page displaying all nine shades with names and descriptions.
• Products page detailing the three formulas, pricing, and shade availability.
• About page covering brand story, values, and key stats.
• Shop page with an order-request form (formula, shade, contact and shipping details) and live price summary.
• Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
• One round of visual design revisions and one round of content revisions.
4.2 Out of Scope (Phase 2 candidates)
• Live payment processing / e-commerce checkout (the Phase 1 form is an order request, not a payment gateway).
• Customer accounts, order history, or login functionality.
• Inventory management or stock syncing.
• Multi-language support.
• Blog or content marketing pages.
5. Deliverables
• Approved sitemap and wireframes.
• Final visual design (colour palette, typography, key components).
• Five fully built, responsive web pages, linked via consistent navigation.
• Working order-request form with live price calculation.
• Basic testing report covering major browsers and devices.
• Live, published website connected to the client's domain.
6. Timeline & Milestones
The project is planned over 8 weeks from sign-off. Timelines assume content (shade names, product copy, pricing) is provided by Week 5.
Phase	Key Activities	Owner	Duration
1. Initiation	Confirm brand direction, scope, budget and success criteria; sign off this proposal	Project Lead / Client	Week 1
2. Planning & Design	Sitemap, wireframes, shade palette, content list, visual style exploration	Designer	Weeks 2–3
3. Build	Develop Home, Shades, Products, About and Shop pages; connect order form logic	Developer	Weeks 4–6
4. Content & Product Setup	Load shade names/descriptions, product copy, pricing, images	Client / Content Owner	Weeks 5–6
5. Testing & QA	Cross-device testing, link checks, form testing, performance check	Developer / QA	Week 7
6. Launch	Domain connection, go-live, post-launch monitoring	Project Lead	Week 8
7. Roles & Responsibilities
Project Lead:  Owns timeline, communication, and final sign-off coordination
Designer:  Produces wireframes, visual design, and shade/product styling
Developer:  Builds and tests all pages, implements order form logic
Client / Content Owner:  Supplies shade names, product copy, pricing, and approves each milestone

9. Risks & Mitigation
Risk	Likelihood	Impact	Mitigation
Delayed content/product photos from client	Medium	Medium	Set content deadlines early; use placeholders to keep build moving
Scope creep (extra pages/features requested mid-build)	Medium	High	Lock scope at sign-off; log new requests as change requests
Browser/device compatibility issues	Low	Medium	Test across major browsers and screen sizes before launch
Payment/checkout requirements change post-launch	Medium	Medium	Launch with order-request form; scope full checkout as phase 2
10. Success Criteria
• All five pages are live, responsive, and free of broken links by the agreed launch date.
• The order-request form successfully captures and confirms customer submissions.
• The client can independently update shade names, prices, and copy going forward (or via agreed support arrangement).
• Site loads within acceptable performance thresholds on mobile devices.

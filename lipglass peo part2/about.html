Why Glaze Gloss

We got tired of choosing.

Shine or comfort. Color or care. We built a formula that stopped making us pick.

Two years, one base formula.

Most glosses make you choose: glassy shine that turns your lips into a glue trap, or a comfortable wear that looks flat by lunch. We spent two years on a base that does both — squalane and vitamin E for slip, a light-bending resin for the shine.

Every batch is mixed by hand in small runs, tested on real lips (ours, mostly), and never on animals. What ships is what we wear every day.
